## 📌 Project Background

This project showcase my skills as a **Data Analyst** working at a fictional healthcare company named "HealthFirst Analytics", which operates in the Healthcare Analytics industry. Founded in 2019, the company specializes in providing data-driven insights to improve patient outcomes, optimize resource allocation, and enhance hospital operational efficiency.

**Industry:** Healthcare Analytics

**Active Years:** 05/2015 - 05/2024

**Business Model:** Data consultancy for healthcare institutions, focusing on:
- Predictive analytics
- Patient outcome monitoring
- Operational efficiency.

**Key Metrics:** Patient demographics, medical conditions, test results, high-risk patient identification, and admission types.

The goal of this project is to analyze patient outcome data to uncover trends in abnormal test results, identify high-risk patients, and provide actionable insights for healthcare providers.

# 🗂 Data Structure 

**Main Dataset (Patient Records):**

**Total Records: 16,383**

**Key Fields:**

- Demographics: Name, Age, Gender, Blood Type
    
- Medical Information: Medical Condition, Doctor, Hospital, Insurance Provider  
  
- Admission Details: Admission Type, Room Number, Length of Stay
    
- Test Results: Medication, Test Results (Normal, Inconclusive, Abnormal)

- Risk Identification: High-Risk Flag based on abnormal test results and age

# 📊 Insights Deep Dive

1. **Patient Demographics & Medical Conditions**
   

- Cancer and Arthritis patients have the highest number of abnormal test results.
  
- Gender Distribution: Abnormal results are almost evenly split between male and female patients, with slight variations based on medical condition.

- Blood Type Correlation: Patients with B+ and O+ blood types show marginally higher abnormal test rates.

2. **Test Results Analysis**
   
- 34% of all tests resulted in abnormal outcomes.

# 📢  Recommendations

**1. Enhanced Monitoring for High-Risk Age Groups (81+):**

- Proactive health programs for high-risk elderly patients.

  
**2. Targeted Chronic Disease Programs:**

- Focus on hypertension and diabetes management to reduce abnormal outcomes.


**3. Optimize Emergency Resources:**

- Allocate more diagnostic resources to emergency admissions based on higher abnormal result rates.

**4. Blood Type Research:**
  - Investigate the correlation between blood types (B+ and O+) and abnormal test results for potential genetic factors.
